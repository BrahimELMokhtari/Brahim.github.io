01 — ABOUT

Research-driven.
Simulation-focused.

I am Brahim El Mokhtari, a researcher in electrical, electronic and systems engineering. My Ph.D. work at Université Clermont Auvergne focused on source identification approaches for electromagnetic compatibility and signal-integrity issues, with an opening toward machine-learning models.

My background also includes nuclear physics, radiation technologies, scientific computing, electromagnetic simulation and software development.
